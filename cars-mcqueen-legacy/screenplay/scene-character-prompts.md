## 一、角色资产

### 1.1 闪电麦昆·退役版（三视图）

```
3D Pixar-style character design sheet. Lightning McQueen from Cars, retired veteran version. Red race car with number 95 on doors, Rust-eze sponsor decals, slightly weathered and faded paint with fine scratches and small chips showing years of racing. Headlights have a warm, wise expression — gentle half-smile, crow's-feet wrinkles around the eyes. Chrome bumper slightly dulled. Lightning bolt decal on sides partially worn. Tires with visible tread wear. Overall impression: a champion who has lived fully. Three-view layout (front, 3/4 angle, side). Clean white background, soft studio lighting with warm highlights. 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

**验收标准：**
- [ ] 95号+Rust-eze标志清晰可辨
- [ ] 车漆有岁月痕迹（细微划痕、褪色）但不破旧
- [ ] 眼神温暖智慧，非疲惫
- [ ] 三视图角度一致
