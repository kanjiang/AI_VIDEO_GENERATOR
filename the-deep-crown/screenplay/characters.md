# THE DEEP CROWN — Character Bible

---

## CHARACTER 1 — MAREN

### Basics
- **Age:** 25 (human appearance)
- **Role in the story:** Protagonist / Hero
- **Profession / position:** Crown Princess of the Deep Kingdom; undercover as a marine logistics analyst at Abyssal Dynamics
- **Place of origin:** The Deep Kingdom, an ancient underwater civilization in the Atlantic trench

### Appearance
- First frame: sleek, athletic build. Moves with an unnatural fluid grace that makes people stare without knowing why. Dark hair, sea-green eyes that catch light oddly.
- Distinguishing features: a faint iridescent sheen on her skin in certain light — nearly invisible but there. A thin scar along her collarbone (from a ritual marking).
- Dress code: Underwater — minimal, ceremonial armor of dark shell and bioluminescent thread. On land — sharp, plain, functional clothes. Dark tones. No jewelry. She dresses to disappear, not to attract.

### Psychology
- **Hamartia:** Over-responsibility. She believes no one else can carry the weight, so she carries everything alone. In normal life this makes her a natural leader. In crisis it makes her refuse help until it's too late.
- **Want:** Destroy the human threat and return home.
- **Need:** Learn that protecting her people does not require sacrificing herself entirely — that trust is also a form of strength.
- **Fear:** That her people will be erased from existence and no one will even know they existed.
- **What she cannot forgive herself for:** Her younger sister Sable was injured in the first border attack. Maren was on a diplomatic mission elsewhere. She was not there.
- **What she cannot forgive others for:** Inaction. Her father's strategy of "hide deeper" is, to her, a passive death sentence.

### Biography
Maren was raised as the heir to the Deep Kingdom's coral throne. From age ten she trained in combat, navigation, and the old sciences. She was not born gentle — she was born capable, and the kingdom shaped her into a weapon disguised as a queen-to-be. Her mother, the previous queen, died during a seismic event when Maren was fifteen. Maren's father withdrew into caution after that — and Maren decided she would never lose anyone again by being cautious.

She has never seen the surface world before this mission. Everything she knows about humans comes from intercepted radio signals and recovered debris. She is walking into the unknown armed with intelligence but zero lived experience.

### Arc
- **Starting point:** A princess who believes "I go" is always the right answer.
- **Reversals:** Her solo infiltration inadvertently leads the enemy to her kingdom's coordinates. The very thing she trusted most — her own competence — becomes the weapon used against her people.
- **Final point:** She saves her people, but can never return. She learns that protecting sometimes means letting go of belonging.

### Voice
- **Line length:** Short. Declarative. She does not explain herself.
- **Register:** Formal by default, becomes clipped and cold under pressure.
- **Slang:** None. She speaks like someone who learned English from intercepted broadcasts — grammatically perfect, slightly too precise.
- **Self-positioning:** Never justifies. States, then acts.
- **Silence:** She is silent when others expect gratitude or emotional response. Her silences unsettle people.
- **Patterns:** "I'll handle it." / Uses the word "we" only when referring to her people, never for casual groups.
- **Meta-humor:** Occasionally makes dry, cutting references to "the old mermaid stories" — in the Deep Kingdom, the tale of a mermaid who sacrificed herself for a human prince and dissolved into foam is a well-known cautionary fable. Maren treats it the way a modern woman treats a sexist fairy tale: with contempt. These lines are delivered deadpan, never played for comedy — they are declarations of who she refuses to be.

**Sample lines:**
1. "I didn't come here to understand them. I came here to stop them."
2. "You're asking me to wait. My people don't have 'wait.'"
3. "I'll handle it."
4. (To Jonas, flatly) "You don't need to know why."
5. (Final act, to herself) "One more door. Then it's done."
6. (When Jonas asks if she's ever been in love with a human) "There was a girl once. Gave up her voice, her tail, her whole identity — for a man who didn't even recognize her. Turned into sea foam. We tell that story to children. As a warning."
7. (When cornered and someone suggests she sacrifice herself) "I'm not that mermaid. I don't dissolve. I detonate."
8. (To Sable, via comms, when things go wrong) "If I end up as sea foam, you have my permission to laugh."

### Relationships
- **Warm with:** Sable (her sister — the only person she shows softness to, via brief underwater communications)
- **Cold with:** Dr. Wexler (controlled hostility masked as professional compliance)
- **Tense with:** Jonas (she needs him but cannot trust him; he asks too many questions)
- **Contradictory with:** King Nereus (she loves her father but despises his passivity)

### Unique forms of address
- Calls her father "King" in arguments, "Father" only in private moments.
- Never uses Jonas's first name until the final act.
- Refers to Wexler only as "him" or "the CEO" when speaking to Sable.

### Objects
- A small shell pendant, sealed with bioluminescent fluid from the kingdom. It glows faintly when she's near saltwater. As her transformation becomes irreversible, the glow fades. When it goes dark entirely, she knows she can never go back.

## CHARACTER 2 — DR. COLE WEXLER

### Basics
- **Age:** 52
- **Role in the story:** Antagonist / Shadow
- **Profession / position:** CEO and founder of Abyssal Dynamics
- **Place of origin:** Boston, Massachusetts

### Appearance
- First frame: silver-haired, lean, impeccably groomed. The kind of man who looks like a TED Talk speaker — warm smile, dead eyes.
- Distinguishing features: always wears a watch with a deep blue face. Hands that are too still when he speaks.
- Dress code: Expensive casual — never a suit, always a cashmere sweater or a linen shirt. He dresses to signal "I'm not a threat." He is the biggest threat in the room.

### Psychology
- **Hamartia:** Obsession with legacy. He doesn't want money — he wants to be the man who "discovered a new species and changed human evolution." He sees himself as a visionary, not a predator.
- **Want:** Capture and decode mermaid regenerative biology to create the next generation of bioweapons — and be remembered as the man who bridged the species gap.
- **Need:** (He will never fulfill this) To accept that some things are not meant to be owned or understood.
- **Fear:** Irrelevance. Being forgotten. Dying as "just another billionaire."
- **What he cannot forgive himself for:** Nothing. He has never once felt guilt. That is his true monstrosity.
- **What he cannot forgive others for:** Disloyalty. Anyone who questions his vision is erased from his world.

### Biography
Wexler was a marine biologist at MIT who stumbled onto anomalous sonar readings twenty years ago. He spent a decade building Abyssal Dynamics under the cover of deep-sea energy research, funded by a mix of defense contracts and private wealth. Five years ago, his unmanned probes captured the first footage of a humanoid aquatic being. Since then, his entire operation has pivoted to one goal: live capture, gene extraction, weaponization. He believes he is doing humanity's work. He sleeps well at night.

### Arc
- **Starting point:** In total control. The board trusts him. The technology works. He is weeks away from a successful capture.
- **Reversals:** He realizes there's an infiltrator — and rather than panic, he uses her as a tracking beacon. He's winning even when he appears to be losing.
- **Final point:** His empire collapses in a single night. Everything destroyed. He survives physically but his legacy is ash.

### Voice
- **Line length:** Medium to long. He enjoys talking. He fills silence.
- **Register:** Educated, warm, avuncular. Uses "we" constantly to create false intimacy.
- **Slang:** None — but uses folksy metaphors. "You can't put the ocean in a jar, but you can sure learn a lot trying."
- **Self-positioning:** Always frames himself as reasonable. "I just want to understand." "We're scientists here."
- **Silence:** Rarely silent. When he goes quiet, it means he already knows the answer and is watching you lie.
- **Patterns:** "Isn't that fascinating?" / "We're so close." / Calls everyone by first name immediately.

**Sample lines:**
1. "I don't want to hurt anything. I want to understand it. And understanding — that requires proximity."
2. "Maren — can I call you Maren? — you have a quality I admire. You don't waste words."
3. "We're so close. Decades of work. I won't let proximity be mistaken for cruelty."
4. (Revealing his knowledge) "Did you really think I wouldn't notice something... extraordinary... walking through my door?"
5. "The ocean doesn't belong to anyone. Including them."

### Relationships
- **Warm with:** No one genuinely. He performs warmth as a tool.
- **Cold with:** Board members who question budgets.
- **Tense with:** Maren — he is fascinated by her from day one. Not romantically. The way a collector is fascinated by a specimen.
- **Contradictory with:** Jonas — he mentored Jonas, but will discard him the moment Jonas becomes inconvenient.

### Objects
- The blue-faced watch. He checks it when he's calculating, not when he's checking time. It's a tell — when you see his eyes drop to the watch, he's already three moves ahead.

## CHARACTER 3 — JONAS HALE

### Basics
- **Age:** 31
- **Role in the story:** Shapeshifter / Gray Ally
- **Profession / position:** Senior marine biologist and gene-mapping lead at Abyssal Dynamics
- **Place of origin:** Portland, Oregon

### Appearance
- First frame: unshaven, tired eyes, perpetually rumpled lab coat. Looks like a man who used to believe in what he does and is now running on inertia.
- Distinguishing features: always has a coffee cup. Fidgets with his pen when thinking.
- Dress code: Functional. Lab coat, jeans, sneakers. Does not care about appearance. His carelessness is honest — he's the only person in the building who looks like what he actually is.

### Psychology
- **Hamartia:** Moral cowardice. He senses something is wrong but lacks the courage to act alone. He needs someone else to go first.
- **Want:** To keep his job, his funding, his research — without having to face what it's being used for.
- **Need:** To finally take a stand even when it costs him everything.
- **Fear:** That he's already complicit in something unforgivable.
- **What he cannot forgive himself for:** He saw the first live capture footage. He said nothing. He told himself it was "for science."
- **What he cannot forgive others for:** Being asked to choose sides. He wants the comfort of ambiguity.

### Biography
Jonas was Wexler's star student at MIT. Brilliant in gene-mapping, naive in politics. Wexler recruited him with promises of "pure research." For five years Jonas told himself the ethical lines hadn't been crossed yet. But the lines kept moving. He's at the point where leaving means admitting he was wrong all along — and he's not brave enough for that. Until Maren arrives and forces the question he's been dodging.

### Arc
- **Starting point:** Complicit through silence. Uncomfortable but passive.
- **Reversals:** He suspects Maren isn't human. Instead of reporting her, he hesitates — and that hesitation is the first real choice he's made in years.
- **Final point:** He helps Maren escape the facility collapse. Not out of love — out of the first genuine moral act of his life. He finally stands for something.

### Voice
- **Line length:** Medium. Trails off. Starts sentences he doesn't finish.
- **Register:** Casual, academic. Uses science jargon as a shield.
- **Slang:** Mild — "yeah," "look," "I mean—"
- **Self-positioning:** Always qualifying. "I'm not saying—" "It's not like I—" "Look, I just—"
- **Silence:** Frequent. His silences are not powerful like Maren's — they are the silence of a man who doesn't have the guts to speak.
- **Patterns:** "I mean—" / "It's complicated." / Deflects hard questions with "That's above my pay grade."

**Sample lines:**
1. "I'm not — look, I just map genes. What they do with it after isn't—" (trails off)
2. "You're not who you say you are. I'm not going to ask. But I'm not going to pretend either."
3. "It's complicated." (said too many times)
4. (Final act) "It's not complicated. It never was. I was just a coward."
5. "Go. I'll hold the door."

### Relationships
- **Warm with:** No one at the company. Isolated by his own fence-sitting.
- **Cold with:** Security staff (they represent the violence he pretends isn't there).
- **Tense with:** Maren — she sees through his excuses instantly and it infuriates/shames him.
- **Contradictory with:** Wexler — he still calls him "Cole," still has loyalty reflexes, even while knowing the man is a monster.

### Objects
- His access badge. He keeps it on a lanyard he fidgets with constantly. In the climax, he uses that badge to open the door for Maren. The thing that kept him complicit becomes the tool of his redemption.

## CHARACTER 4 — SABLE

### Basics
- **Age:** 20 (human appearance equivalent)
- **Role in the story:** Herald / Ally / Emotional anchor
- **Profession / position:** Younger princess; communications specialist
- **Place of origin:** The Deep Kingdom

### Appearance
- Seen only via underwater holographic communication in most of the film. Lighter, more expressive than Maren. Faster eyes, quicker smile.
- Distinguishing features: a still-healing scar across her shoulder from the first border attack.
- Dress code: Kingdom warrior-trainee attire — lighter and less ceremonial than Maren's.

### Psychology
- **Hamartia:** Idolization of her sister. She trusts Maren's judgment absolutely — which means she enables Maren's lone-wolf behavior instead of challenging it.
- **Want:** For Maren to come home safe.
- **Need:** To become her own leader, not just Maren's relay.
- **Fear:** That Maren won't come back.

### Biography
The younger sister. She was injured in the first border attack — the event that drove Maren's guilt and departure. Sable stays behind to maintain the communication link and manage their father. She is the emotional bridge between Maren and the world Maren is fighting for.

### Arc
- **Starting point:** Loyal support. "I'll keep the line open."
- **Final point:** She surfaces at the end — not to bring Maren home, but to see her sister one last time. The one who inherits the crown.

### Voice
- **Line length:** Short but warmer than Maren's. More emotional.
- **Register:** Informal for royalty. She speaks to Maren like a sister, not a subject.
- **Patterns:** "Mare." (her nickname for Maren) / "Come back." / "I'm fine. Stop."

**Sample lines:**
1. "Mare. It's getting worse down here. Father won't listen."
2. "Come back. Just — come back."
3. (Final scene, across the water) No words. Just a nod.

### Objects
- A matching shell pendant to Maren's — hers still glows.

## CHARACTER 5 — KING NEREUS

### Basics
- **Age:** 55 (human equivalent)
- **Role in the story:** Threshold Guardian / Mentor (failed)
- **Profession / position:** King of the Deep Kingdom
- **Place of origin:** The Deep Kingdom

### Appearance
- Massive, once-powerful. Now carries himself with the weight of someone who chose retreat over fight. Eyes that used to command now only worry.
- Distinguishing features: a crown of black coral he no longer wears in private.

### Psychology
- **Hamartia:** Grief calcified into paralysis. After losing his queen, he decided the only way to protect his people is to hide. He mistakes retreat for wisdom.
- **Want:** To keep his daughters alive — at any cost, including the cost of their freedom.
- **Need:** To trust his daughter's strength instead of shielding her from the world.
- **Fear:** Losing another family member to the surface world.

### Arc
- **Starting point:** "We go deeper. We hide. We survive."
- **Reversals:** His plan to attack the surface facility nearly destroys everything — Maren has to stop him remotely.
- **Final point:** He lets go. He accepts Maren's choice. He crowns Sable.

### Voice
- **Line length:** Long, deliberate. Speaks like a king even in private.
- **Register:** Elevated, formal.
- **Patterns:** "I will not lose—" / "The surface is death." / Addresses Maren as "daughter" never by name when angry.

**Sample lines:**
1. "The surface is death. Your mother knew that. I know that. Why don't you?"
2. "I will not lose another one of mine to their world."
3. (Final) "Go well, daughter. The crown passes."

### Objects
- The black coral crown. He stops wearing it after Maren leaves — and places it on Sable's head in the final scene.
