# THE MAP

## Episode 01 — 照片上的地方

**Written for:** AI animation short series (Pixar-style family audience)  
**Target runtime:** ~2:30  
**Episode function:** Establish why Momo wants to leave and why it has not left before.  
**Value shift:** Dream trapped in a garage → dream becomes a road.  

---

## CHARACTER VOICES

| Character | Voice | Speech Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Momo | Young boy, ~6-8 years old, soft and timid | Hesitant, many pauses, small voice when scared, suddenly clear when choosing |

---

## SCENE 1 — THE PHOTO

**INT. MOMO'S GARAGE — MORNING**

A small garage door sits half-open. Sunlight stops at the floor line.

MOMO, a tiny blue hatchback, idles in the shade.

On the wall: an old photo. A golden valley. Cars splash through a stream. Others race over grass.

Momo rolls closer.

One tire traces a dusty road on the garage floor. The line curves toward the door.

The tire stops at the sunlight.

Momo looks up at the photo.

MOMO: "今天...也只是看看。"

A soft gust lifts one corner of the photo.

Momo nudges it flat with the bumper.

Behind the photo, tape has left old marks on the wall. This photo has been there a long time.

---

## SCENE 2 — THE ROUNDABOUT

**EXT. MOMO'S GARAGE / SMALL TOWN ROUNDABOUT — MORNING**

Momo inches forward until the front wheels touch sunlight.

Outside, BIG CARS whip around the roundabout: a bus, a pickup, a red sports car.

The sports car BLASTS past Momo's door.

Momo jolts backward.

MOMO: "太...太快了！"

A bus horn BOOMS.

Momo reverses into the garage. The door rattles halfway down.

From inside the shade, Momo looks at the photo.

MOMO: "我想去。"

A beat.

MOMO: "可是我不敢。"

---

## SCENE 3 — THE MAP ARRIVES

**EXT. GARAGE ENTRANCE — LATER**

Wind runs down the street.

Leaves spin through the roundabout.

A passing delivery truck loses a folded paper from its rear rack.

The paper flips through the air.

It slaps onto Momo's windshield.

MOMO: "哎？这是什么？"

Momo's wipers SWIPE once.

The paper stays.

The wipers SWIPE again.

The paper opens.

It is a hand-drawn map. A road leaves a little roundabout, climbs through mountains, crosses a bridge, and ends at a golden circle.

Momo stares.

MOMO: "这个圆圈..."

Momo backs into the garage, turns toward the wall photo, then looks back at the map.

The mountain shape in the photo matches the mountain shape on the map.

Momo's headlights widen.

MOMO: "是照片上的地方。"

---

## SCENE 4 — THE BACK OF THE PHOTO

**INT. MOMO'S GARAGE — CONTINUOUS**

The old tape at the top of the photo finally loosens.

A soft gust flips the photo outward. It hangs from the lower strip of tape, back side facing Momo.

On the back, faded handwriting:

“有一天，路会来找你。”

Momo reads it once.

The garage is quiet.

Momo looks at the map on the windshield.

Momo looks at the writing.

MOMO: "我不是不想去..."

Momo swallows with a tiny engine sputter.

MOMO: "我只是...不知道路。"

Momo eases forward and gently nudges the photo's lower edge. The last strip of tape gives way.

The photo slides down onto Momo's hood.

Momo lifts one windshield wiper, then lets it press the photo against the left side of the windshield.

The map stays under the right wiper.

---

## SCENE 5 — THE EXIT

**EXT. ROUNDABOUT — MORNING**

Momo stops at the roundabout exit.

LEFT: the familiar loop. The same garages. The same quiet shade.

RIGHT: an open road toward the mountains.

The old photo rests under the left wiper.

The map trembles under the right wiper.

Momo's wheels turn left.

Stop.

Turn right.

Stop.

MOMO: "如果路真的来了..."

A red car speeds around the roundabout behind Momo.

Momo flinches.

The engine almost dies.

Silence.

Momo looks at the photo.

In the old photo, several cars pull a smaller car from the mud. Momo does not notice the detail yet.

Momo's engine catches.

MOMO: "那我就试一下。"

Momo drives past the roundabout exit.

The town shrinks in the rearview mirror.

The photo shakes under the left wiper.

The map points forward.

---

## SCENE 6 — FIRST ROAD

**EXT. MOUNTAIN ROAD — DAY**

Momo climbs a narrow road.

The town is far below.

The road curves into trees.

Momo slows, looks back once, then keeps going.

MOMO: "不要回头。"

A tiny horn echoes from below the rocks.

BEEP-BEEP-BEEP.

Momo stops.

The horn sounds again.

BEEP-BEEP-BEEP.

Momo looks at the map. The route points forward.

The horn comes from a side path.

Momo's wheels turn toward the sound.

CUT TO BLACK.

## STRUCTURAL AUDIT

| Scene | Function | Value In → Value Out | Causality |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — The Photo | Establish desire | Dream → trapped dream | Momo already wants the valley before the map arrives |
| 2 — The Roundabout | Establish fear | Attempt → retreat | Outside speed proves why Momo has not left |
| 3 — The Map Arrives | Inciting incident | Dream image → usable route | Map matches the photo, turning longing into action |
| 4 — Back of Photo | Recognition | Passive longing → personal call | The message makes the route feel meant for Momo |
| 5 — The Exit | Threshold choice | Fear → first courage | Momo chooses to try, not because fear is gone |
| 6 — First Road | Hook / moral test setup | Solo journey → possible interruption | Momo must choose between map route and someone calling |

### Why This Version Works

The episode no longer depends on a random map to motivate Momo. The photo proves Momo has always wanted the valley; the roundabout proves why it stayed; the map provides the route; the back-of-photo message converts the route into a personal call. The ending sets up Episode 02 by forcing Momo to choose between following the map and answering another vehicle's need.
