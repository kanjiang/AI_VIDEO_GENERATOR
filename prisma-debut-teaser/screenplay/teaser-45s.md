# PRISMA — DEBUT TEASER (45s) — v1

Format: Hollywood-style action screenplay for a 45-second music-video teaser.
Rules: action verbs only. No mood adjectives. Time codes in brackets.

---

**FADE IN:**

**INT. VOID STUDIO — TIMELESS**  [00:00–00:03]

BLACK.

A low HEARTBEAT.

A single WHITE BEAM stabs down from above and HITS a floating crystal prism.

**INT. VOID STUDIO — TIMELESS**  [00:03–00:06]

The beam SPLITS into FIVE COLORED SHAFTS — blue, pink, white, gold, orange.

Each shaft LANDS on a floor mark. Five SILHOUETTES stand in five colors.

GLASS CRACK. Beat drops.

---

**INT. VOID STUDIO — BLUE POOL**  [00:06–00:11]

The BLUE SILHOUETTE turns. LAN (China, high ponytail, modern qipao) STEPS into the blue light.

She SNAPS a folding fan open at eye level.

She PIVOTS on one heel — full 360° spin. High ponytail WHIPS through blue light. Qipao hem TRAILS.

Ends facing camera. Fan LOWERS in one beat.

                              LAN
                        (Mandarin, low)
                        Zài zhèlǐ.
                        (I'm here.)

CHYRON, bottom-left: **LAN · 岚 · CHINA**

**INT. VOID STUDIO — PINK POOL**  [00:11–00:16]

The PINK SILHOUETTE spins on the heel. YEON (Korea, short hanbok top over high-waist mini) WHIPS her long sleeve across frame.

Mid-spin, she DROPS into a deep back-bend — one knee grounded, chest arched, sleeve trailing pink light overhead.

She SNAPS upright in one beat. Sleeve settles.

FREEZE at 45°. Eye contact.

                              YEON
                        (Korean, sharp)
                        Naya.
                        (It's me.)

CHYRON: **YEON · 燕 · KOREA**

**INT. VOID STUDIO — WHITE POOL**  [00:16–00:21]

SKYE (USA, oversized bomber, boots) STRIDES forward.

She TOSSES her cap up.

DROPS into a one-hand freeze — legs stacked over her shoulder, opposite hand pointed at camera.

The cap LANDS back on her head, reversed.

She RISES in one beat. Chin down. Finger COCKED at camera.

                              SKYE
                        (English, fast)
                        Right here.

CHYRON: **SKYE · 星 · USA**

**INT. VOID STUDIO — GOLD POOL**  [00:21–00:26]

MALI (Thailand, simplified chada crown, gold dance dress) HOLDS a Thai classical hand pose. Slow.

Wrist ROLLS. Fingers ARCH.

Beat KICKS.

She LAUNCHES into an aerial spin — one leg tucked, gold fabric FLARES.

LANDS in a low lunge. Shoulders POP-LOCK — twice. Head SNAPS to camera.

                              MALI
                        (Thai, breathy)
                        Nêe chǎn.
                        (This is me.)

CHYRON: **MALI · 曼 · THAILAND**

**INT. VOID STUDIO — ORANGE POOL**  [00:26–00:31]

RANI (India, sari fabric, half veil, henna hands) TILTS her head.

The veil SLIDES down her face — one eye REVEALED.

CUT — CLOSE ON THE EYE. Kohl-lined. She BLINKS once.

BACK WIDE. She DROPS into araimandi (knees bent 90°, feet turned out).

STOMPS the floor — twice. Ankle bells RING sharp.

Both arms SNAP out into a T. Fingers CURLED into mudra.

FREEZE.

                              RANI
                        (Hindi, warm)
                        Main aayi.
                        (I've arrived.)

CHYRON: **RANI · 兰妮 · INDIA**

**INT. VOID STUDIO — FIVE POOLS**  [00:31–00:36]

Five WIDE SHOTS, side by side in split-frame.

All five WALK forward from their pools of color.

The pools OVERLAP at center.

---

**INT. VOID STUDIO — CENTER**  [00:36–00:41]

They MEET. Form a V. LAN center. YEON and SKYE flanking. MALI and RANI on the wings.

They TURN. FACE camera. FREEZE.

The five colored beams CONVERGE on the floor at their feet.

MICRO-SILENCE. 0.5 second.

                              ALL FIVE
                        (SUNG unison, F#m ascending hook, 5s)
                        We are —— PRIS —— MA!

Held sustain on "MA" for 3 seconds.

---

**INT. VOID STUDIO — CENTER**  [00:41–00:44]

Over the sustained final note:

The five colors on the floor BLEED into each other and BURN back to WHITE.

WHITE BLOOM fills the frame.

---

**BLACK.**  [00:44–00:45]

Text SLAMS onto screen:

                        **PRISMA**
                        DEBUT · 2026

Single low BOOM.

**CUT TO BLACK.**

## RUNTIME AUDIT

- Cold open: 6s
- Five signatures × 5s = 25s
- Merge + group pose: 5s (0:31-0:36)
- Micro-silence: 0.5s (0:36-0:36.5)
- Sung hook "We are PRIS-MA": 5s (0:36.5-0:41.5, sustained "MA" overlaps into next block)
- Color bleed to white + bloom (over hook sustain): ~2.5s
- Logo slam: 1s
- **Total: 45s ✓**

## CUT-TO-30s NOTES

If we need a 30-second version:
- Kill the split-frame walk (00:31–00:36)
- Trim each signature moment from 5s to 3.5s (cut the beat before each line)
- Keep cold open, all five lines, group pose, logo
- Result: ~30s

## WHY THIS WORKS

**One reason:** every design choice is locked to the prism metaphor — the color per member, the pool of light per member, the merge back to white — so the "five nations" concept never reads as pasted-together mascots. The audience gets ONE image (prism splits light) and everything else is a variation on it. That's what makes a debut teaser memorable in 45 seconds.
