# 《我的专属云朵》角色与场景资产提示词

## 使用说明

以下提示词用于生成参考图资产（角色/场景/道具），在 Nano Banana / Soul / 即梦 / MidJourney 等工具中生成后，将图片命名并上传用于视频生成时挂载。

## 角色

### 小夏（主角）

```
3D Pixar-style character design, turnaround sheet.
A Chinese girl around 6-7 years old, round face with rosy cheeks, black hair tied in two short pigtails with fluffy cloud-shaped white hair ties, bright curious eyes with long eyelashes, fair skin with a light sunburn on nose and cheeks from playing outdoors.
Wearing a light blue sleeveless dress with a small cloud pattern print, white knee-length leggings, pink jelly sandals.
Expression: default cheerful grin showing one missing front tooth, dimples visible when smiling.
Proportions: slightly stylized large head (head-to-body ratio about 1:3.5), chubby limbs, slightly shorter and rounder than average.
Render: Pixar-level subsurface scattering on skin, soft rim lighting, neutral gray background for asset clarity.
```

文件命名：`小夏.png`

### 团团（云朵伙伴）

```
3D Pixar-style character design, turnaround sheet.
A small sentient cloud, roughly the size of a large beanbag (about 1.2 meters wide, 0.7 meters tall). Soft fluffy cumulus shape with gently rounded edges, semi-translucent white with subtle pearlescent iridescence.
Two round dark-blue eyes with soft highlights (like water droplets), a tiny curved smile that shifts shape to express emotions. No nose, no limbs — the cloud reshapes parts of its body to gesture (a puffy arm-like extension when waving, a flat canopy when shielding).
Floating height: default at about 2 meters above ground, bobs gently like breathing.
Surface: cotton-candy texture with volumetric softness, subtle internal glow in warm sunlight, turns slightly gray-blue when producing rain.
Expression sheet: happy (eyes curve into crescents, body puffs up), protective (body flattens wide like an umbrella, eyes determined), sleepy (eyes half-closed, body sinks lower and flattens into a mattress shape), playful (body bounces, small cloud puffs shoot off edges).
Render: volumetric cloud rendering, subsurface light scattering, soft shadow on ground, neutral gray background.
```

文件命名：`团团.png`

## 场景

### 夏日街道

```
3D Pixar-style environment. A quiet residential street in a small Chinese city during peak summer. Blazing white sunlight, heat haze shimmering above asphalt. A row of mid-rise apartment buildings with balconies (laundry hanging, potted plants). A few trees with dark green canopies casting sharp shadows. One red sedan parked on the roadside, dusty and sun-baked. Pavement so bright it's almost white. Clear blue sky with only a few wispy clouds very high up. Temperature feeling: scorching. A popsicle wrapper on the ground. Cicada sound implied by the stillness. No people visible.
Wide establishing shot, slight low angle to emphasize the oppressive sky.
```
