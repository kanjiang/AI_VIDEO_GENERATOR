## TREATMENT VALIDATION PRINCIPLES

After filling it out, go scene by scene and answer:

1. **Does every scene begin with "because"?** Scene N happens because scene N-1 produced X.
2. **Does every scene move a value** from positive to negative or vice versa?
3. **Does every scene serve the project's controlling idea?**
4. **If you remove the scene, does the film change?** If not, the scene is expendable.

If even one answer is "no," rewrite or remove the scene.

---

## AUDIT TAGS

When you find a problem, tag it:

- ⚠ **[CAUSALITY]** — the scene does not follow from the previous one
- ⚠ **[VALUE]** — no value shift
- ⚠ **[BIBLE]** — conflict with the character or world bible
- ⚠ **[PACE]** — too fast or too slow for its function
- ⚠ **[INFO DUMP]** — exposition delivered through dialogue
- ⚠ **[REPETITION]** — repeats what the story already showed
