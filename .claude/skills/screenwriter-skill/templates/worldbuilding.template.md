## RULES OF EXPOSITION

Introduce the world to the audience without direct explanatory dialogue such as "as you remember, this is how our magic works." Show it through action, objects in the frame, and consequences.

A strong tactic is to show the system in action, then show the character treating it as normal. The audience understands that this is ordinary inside the story world.
