# 《地图》Season 01 Treatment

**Format:** 5 episodes × 2-3 minutes  
**Audience:** Family / Pixar-style animation  
**Main Characters:** Momo, Kiki, Bruno  

---

## Season Spine

Momo wants to reach the valley in the old photo, but fear keeps it inside the garage. The map gives Momo a route, yet the journey only becomes meaningful when Momo stops chasing the destination alone and chooses to help Kiki. By the end, Momo earns the valley not because it was brave from the start, but because it learned that courage is something vehicles can share.

---

## Episode 01 — 照片上的地方

**Runtime target:** 2:30  
**Purpose:** Fix Momo's motivation.  
**Value shift:** Dream trapped in a garage → dream becomes a road.  

### Treatment

Momo lives in the last garage beside the town roundabout. Inside the garage, above a shelf of tiny tools, hangs an old sun-faded photo: a golden valley full of cars racing through grass, splashing through a stream, and laughing under trees. Momo studies the photo every morning, traces the valley road with one tire on the dusty floor, then stops at the garage door.

Outside, the roundabout is loud and fast. Big cars cut across lanes, honk, and throw wind against Momo's door. Momo tries to roll out but panics when a sports car blasts past. It backs into the garage and whispers that here is safe.

A gust of wind knocks the old photo loose. As Momo catches it against the wall, a hand-drawn map slaps onto its windshield. The map's final golden circle matches the mountain shape in the old photo. Momo flips the photo over and sees faded handwriting: “有一天，路会来找你.”

Momo reaches the roundabout exit. It looks back at the safe garage, then at the photo held under its left windshield wiper. The engine coughs, almost dies, then steadies. Momo drives past the roundabout for the first time.

### Key Dialogue

MOMO: "我不是不想去...我只是...不知道路。"

MOMO: "这不是随便一个地方。是照片上的地方。"

MOMO: "如果路真的来了...那我就试一下。"

### Ending Hook

Momo climbs the first mountain road. From somewhere below the rocks comes a small, panicked horn: beep-beep-beep.

## Episode 02 — 不是一个人的路

**Runtime target:** 2:30  
**Purpose:** Add relationship and moral choice.  
**Value shift:** Protect the route → protect another vehicle.  

### Treatment

Momo follows the map carefully, keeping the photo visible on the dashboard. The little horn sounds again from a side path. The map points straight ahead; the sound comes from the wrong direction. Momo starts to continue, then stops.

Momo turns off the marked road and finds Kiki, a colorful little scooter, front wheel trapped in a loose gravel ditch. Kiki talks too fast, pretending it meant to stop there. Momo tries to pull Kiki free but only makes the gravel slide more. Kiki panics and finally admits it cannot move.

Momo studies the ground. It notices the gravel shifts only when weight lands on the soft middle. Momo uses the back of the map as a temporary traction pad and positions itself low, letting Kiki's front wheel climb onto firmer stone. The map gets muddy and one route line becomes unreadable.

Kiki is free. Momo is horrified by the damaged map. Kiki insists it knows a shortcut to the valley. Momo does not trust it, but the main line is smeared. They go together.

### Key Dialogue

KIKI: "我没卡住！我只是在...研究石头。"

MOMO: "地图说直走。"

KIKI: "那你干嘛转过来了？"

MOMO: "因为你在叫。"

KIKI: "你把地图弄脏了！"

MOMO: "我知道。可是你出来了。"

### Ending Hook

Kiki points to a narrow mountain road under dark clouds.

KIKI: "快路。"

MOMO: "那片云...看起来不快。看起来很湿。"

---

## Episode 03 — 雨里的快路

**Runtime target:** 2:30  
**Purpose:** Make the friendship active under pressure.  
**Value shift:** I slow you down → I keep you safe.  

### Treatment

Kiki races ahead through the “shortcut,” showing off. Momo follows too slowly, worrying about the wet road. Kiki mocks Momo for braking too much. Rain begins.

The road turns slick. Kiki takes a curve too fast and slides toward the rail. Momo panics, then steers into the skid path and bumps Kiki back toward the road. Momo also slips and nearly hits the rail, but both vehicles recover.

The storm grows. They can barely see. Momo suggests they use their headlights together: Kiki lights the near road, Momo lights farther ahead. Moving slowly as a pair, they find a buried road sign. It confirms they are still heading toward the valley, but another sign warns: OLD BRIDGE AHEAD.

Kiki tries to joke. Momo sees Kiki's wheel shaking. Neither says the word fear.

### Key Dialogue

KIKI: "你这样开，蜗牛都会超车。"

MOMO: "蜗牛至少不会打滑。"

KIKI: "我没事！我能——啊！"

MOMO: "慢一点也能到。"

KIKI: "你刚才...是故意撞我？"

MOMO: "是故意救你。"

### Ending Hook

Lightning reveals an old bridge symbol on the sign. Kiki's voice gets smaller.

KIKI: "桥啊...我最喜欢桥了。"

MOMO: "你说谎的时候，车灯会眨。"

---

## Episode 04 — 我们一起过

**Runtime target:** 3:00  
**Purpose:** Series climax: Momo chooses connection over fear.  
**Value shift:** I cannot cross → we can cross.  

### Treatment

Fog wraps the mountain. Momo and Kiki reach the stone bridge. It is narrow, cracked, and has no rails. Momo freezes before the first stone. Kiki, desperate not to look scared, speeds onto the bridge.

Halfway across, a stone breaks. Kiki's small front wheel drops between two cracked slabs. The bridge groans. Kiki stops joking. Momo stands at the bridge entrance, trembling.

Momo almost reverses. It looks at the old valley photo held under the left windshield wiper. In the photo, several cars are not racing; they are pulling another small car out of mud. Momo realizes it had always looked at the wrong part of the picture. The valley was not about speed. It was about not leaving anyone behind.

Momo inches onto the bridge. It talks Kiki through each tiny move. Kiki admits it is scared. Momo admits it is scared too. The honesty steadies them. Momo braces its side against Kiki, letting Kiki's wheel climb out. They cross together, slowly.

The final wheel touches solid ground. Sunlight breaks through the clouds.

### Key Dialogue

KIKI: "Momo...我真的卡住了。"

MOMO: "我看见了。"

KIKI: "你可以先过去。"

MOMO: "不。"

KIKI: "你不怕吗？"

MOMO: "怕。"

KIKI: "那怎么办？"

MOMO: "我们一起过。"

### Ending Hook

On the other side stands an old tunnel door. The same symbol from the back of the photo is carved into the stone.

---

## Episode 05 — 地图会去找下一个人

**Runtime target:** 3:00  
**Purpose:** Resolve destination, community, and map mythology.  
**Value shift:** I found the valley → I belong because I helped someone arrive.  

### Treatment

Momo and Kiki push through the old tunnel door. Inside, their headlights reveal hundreds of photos pinned along the walls. Every photo shows a different vehicle at the start of a journey: a tractor in a field, a bus at a depot, a tiny van in a snowy town. Each has the same hand-drawn map in the corner.

Momo finds a blank space on the wall. It looks at its own old valley photo and realizes it belongs here now, not as something to stare at from far away, but as part of the story. It tapes the photo to the wall.

The tunnel opens into the sunlit valley. Cars of every shape welcome them. Bruno, the big red truck, recognizes the muddy map and laughs warmly. He says the map never belongs to the first vehicle that finds it. It belongs to the next one who needs a road.

At sunset, Momo and Kiki sit on the ridge. The wind lifts the map from Momo's hood. Kiki starts to chase it, but Momo stops. The map floats back toward town.

Momo watches it go, smiling.

### Key Dialogue

BRUNO: "你们不是第一个找到这张地图的。"

KIKI: "那我们要还给谁？"

BRUNO: "不用还。它自己会走。"

MOMO: "去吧。去找下一个想出发的小家伙。"

### Final Image

Back in town, another small garage sits half-open. A shy little car looks out. The map lands gently on its windshield.

---

## Season Causality Chain

1. Episode 01 happens because Momo already dreams of the valley, and the map gives that dream a route.
2. Episode 02 happens because Momo hears Kiki and chooses a living need over a drawn route.
3. Episode 03 happens because helping Kiki damages the map, forcing them to rely on each other.
4. Episode 04 happens because Kiki's false bravery creates a crisis that Momo can only solve by facing its own fear.
5. Episode 05 happens because crossing together proves Momo understands the valley's real value: shared courage.
