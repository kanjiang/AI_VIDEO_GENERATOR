# THE MAP

**Written for:** AI animation short (Pixar-style family audience)
**Target runtime:** ~2 minutes (120 seconds)
**Controlling Idea:** Life becomes an adventure when a fearful character chooses curiosity over safety, because the unknown rewards those who dare to enter it.

## CHARACTER VOICES

| Character | Voice | Speech Pattern |
|---|---|---|
| Momo | Young boy, ~6-8 years old, soft and timid | Hesitant, many pauses ("..."), stutters when scared, suddenly clear and complete when determined |
| Big Truck | Deep warm adult male | Slow, steady, warm — every word feels grounded |
| Scooter | High-pitched energetic teen | Fast, bouncy, excited, rising intonation |

---

## SCENE 1 — ORDINARY WORLD

**EXT. SMALL TOWN — ROUNDABOUT — DAY**

A row of garages lines a quiet street. In the last garage, a tiny hatchback, MOMO, idles in the dark.

Outside, BIGGER CARS roar through the roundabout — a pickup, a sports car, a bus. Confident. Loud. Fast.

Momo ROLLS forward to the garage door. Peeks out.

MOMO: "外面的世界...好大好快啊..."

A sports car BLASTS past, horn blaring.

MOMO: "太...太快了！"

Momo REVERSES back into shadow. The garage door CLOSES halfway.

MOMO: "还是这里好...这里安全..."

---

## SCENE 2 — CALL TO ADVENTURE

**EXT. GARAGE ENTRANCE — DAY**

A gust of wind RIPS a faded paper off the back of a passing truck. The paper SLAPS onto Momo's windshield.

MOMO: "哎？这是什么？"

Momo's wipers SWIPE — once, twice. The paper holds.

MOMO: "怎么刮不掉..."

It is a hand-drawn map. A winding line climbs through mountains. At the end: a golden circle marked "HERE."

MOMO: "HERE...那个地方...是什么样的呢？"

Momo's headlights FLICKER. Curiosity.

---

## SCENE 3 — REFUSAL AND THRESHOLD

**EXT. ROUNDABOUT — DAY**

Momo sits at the roundabout's exit lane — the one that leads out of town. The map is tucked under one wiper.

LEFT: the familiar loop. The same garages. The same street.

MOMO: "左边...是回家的路。"

RIGHT: an open road stretching toward distant mountains.

MOMO: "右边...是..."

Momo's wheels TURN left. Then stop. TURN right. Stop.

MOMO: "可是...万一..."

A long beat.

Momo's engine REVS. Small. Determined. The exhaust pipe puffs a little cloud.

MOMO: "不管了！我要去看看！"

Momo DRIVES past the roundabout and onto the open road. The town shrinks in the rearview mirror.

---

## SCENE 4A — TEST: RAIN

**EXT. MOUNTAIN ROAD — RAIN — CONTINUOUS**

Dark clouds roll in. Rain HAMMERS the road. Momo's wipers work overtime.

MOMO: "下雨了...路好滑..."

The road curves. Momo SKIDS on the wet surface — tires SCREECH — slides toward the edge.

MOMO: "啊啊啊——！"

Momo CORRECTS. Straightens. Keeps going. The map on the windshield is soaked but still readable.

MOMO: "呼...没事...继续走...继续走..."

---

## SCENE 4B — TEST: FOG

**EXT. MOUNTAIN ROAD — FOG — CONTINUOUS**

Thick fog swallows the road. Momo's headlights barely cut through.

MOMO: "什么都看不见了...前面到底有没有路..."

Momo SLOWS to a crawl. Follows the map's line from memory.

A CLIFF EDGE appears out of nowhere — two feet ahead.

Momo BRAKES. Hard. Gravel sprays over the edge into silence.

MOMO: "差一点...差一点就..."

A beat. Then Momo backs up, finds the road again, and continues.

---

## SCENE 4C — TEST: BRIDGE

**EXT. STONE BRIDGE — GORGE — CONTINUOUS**

A crumbling stone bridge spans a deep gorge. Wind HOWLS through the gap.

MOMO: "这...这座桥...能过吗？"

Momo INCHES onto the bridge. The stones GROAN.

Halfway across, a chunk of stone BREAKS off the edge and FALLS into the gorge. A distant splash.

Momo FREEZES. Engine DIES. Complete silence.

A long beat.

Then Momo's engine FIRES again.

MOMO: "...可以的。"

Momo ROLLS forward, slow and steady, until all four tires reach solid ground.

MOMO: "我过来了...我过来了！"

The storm CLEARS. Sunlight breaks through.

---

## SCENE 5 — REWARD

**EXT. HIDDEN VALLEY — DAY**

The road crests a hill and opens onto a vast, sunlit valley.

MOMO: "哇...这就是...地图上的地方？"

Cars of every shape and color fill the landscape. A rusted truck SPLASHES through a stream. Tiny scooters RACE along a ridge. A van with flowers painted on its side ROLLS through a meadow.

A big truck HONKS a greeting — deep and warm.

BIG TRUCK: "嘿！小家伙！你一个人上来的？"

A scooter pulls alongside, waving a tiny flag.

SCOOTER: "厉害啊！快下来一起玩！"

Momo PAUSES at the top of the hill. Then ACCELERATES down the slope.

MOMO: "来了来了！"

Momo joins the flow. Headlights bright. Wide open.

---

## SCENE 6 — RESOLUTION

**EXT. VALLEY RIDGE — SUNSET**

Momo sits on a ridge, facing the sunset. Behind: the long mountain road winding back toward town.

MOMO: "原来...外面的世界不可怕。"

Momo's headlights FLICKER — a smile.

The wind picks up. The map LIFTS off Momo's hood.

MOMO: "啊，地图——"

The map catches the breeze and FLOATS away — back toward the town, back toward another garage, back toward the next timid car waiting to find it.

MOMO: "...去吧。去找下一个想出发的小家伙。"

**FADE TO BLACK.**

### Causality Check
- Scene 2 happens **because** the wind delivers the map (external catalyst)
- Scene 3 happens **because** the map creates a choice that didn't exist before
- Scene 4 happens **because** Momo chose the unknown road
- Scene 5 happens **because** Momo survived the tests
- Scene 6 happens **because** Momo no longer needs the map — it belongs to the next car

Every scene begins with "because," not "after that." The chain is causal.
