# Project lock — Jam Island Control

This file is the cross-episode production lock for the fruit-world slapstick adventure series. It must be checked before any shotlist, storyboard, scene board, or Seedance prompt is written.

Texture signature for the whole show: **fruit wax highlights, water beads, translucent flesh, syrup stringing**. Every asset carries it. This is what separates this IP from other candy-colored 3D kids animation.

---

## Characters

### Bobo — cherry tomato
- Identity one-liner: small round cherry tomato; the intern who sincerely believes every button is waiting to be pressed by him.
- Body / shape: perfectly round body, very thin skin with strong fruit-wax highlight; short stubby limbs; rolls rather than runs.
- Default design: saturated tomato red; five-point green calyx on top as hair; oversized round black pupils (baby-face ratio); wide elastic mouth line.
- Series marker: green calyx stands straight up when emotionally triggered.
- Expression system: broad and readable. Excitement enlarges pupils and stretches mouth wide. Pre-burst state swells the body until skin turns semi-translucent and the juice inside is visibly sloshing. Post-burst state is a limp empty skin.
- Signature mechanic: emotion spike → swell → **burst** → limp skin → slow re-inflation. He never exits after bursting; he keeps working as a deflated skin.
- Voice / speech texture: high pitched fast boy voice, rising sentence ends, cracks when excited. Deflated state is a hoarse leaking-air whisper that collapses mid-sentence.
- Must match assets: `bobo-identity-board.png`.
- Drift bans: no realistic tomato texture, no beefsteak-tomato proportions, no missing calyx, no small clever pupils, no instant recovery after bursting (deflated stage is mandatory), no blood or wound reading — pulp must stay bright cheerful red.

### Ningqi — lemon
- Identity one-liner: yellow lemon; the duty supervisor who is the only fruit on the island that remembers the last big eruption.
- Body / shape: lemon body slightly pointed at both ends; visible pitted rind texture, never smooth.
- Default design: bright yellow rind with pore-pitted surface; two dark-yellow short brow lines permanently knitted; narrow elongated eyes, much smaller than Bobo's; a small procedure board clipped at her waist.
- Series marker: the procedure board covered in written steps — always present.
- Expression system: narrow range, tension-first. Disapproval knits the brows tighter. Fear appears for only one or two frames and only when she loses control. Never a wide happy smile — the maximum is a slight corner release.
- Signature mechanic: squeezes lemon juice from the corner of her eyes under stress; the higher the pressure the further it sprays. The one thing a control freak cannot control is her own acid.
- Voice / speech texture: mature-leaning neutral female voice; fast and crisp; tight articulation; falling sentence ends; recites procedure like a checklist without breathing.
- Must match assets: `ningqi-identity-board.png`.
- Drift bans: no smooth glossy lemon surface, no open happy laugh, no missing procedure board, no cloudy juice — juice must read clear.

### Guashu — watermelon computer
- Identity one-liner: whole watermelon embedded in the console; the oldest machine on the island; knows every answer but always negotiates first.
- Body / shape: front hemisphere of a whole watermelon set into the control desk. No limbs, never stands.
- Default design: deep green rind with dark-green tiger striping that drifts subtly with mood; the screen is a cut face of bright red flesh; data is displayed as arrangements of black seeds.
- Series marker: seeds are the data — individually legible at all times.
- Expression system: expressed through stripe drift and seed rearrangement rather than a face.
- Signature mechanic: bargains before answering ("say sorry first", "call me Uncle Gua first"). Long boot-up hum before every startup. Dropped seeds equal lost memory.
- Voice / speech texture: low slow elderly male voice with faint electrical noise; always preceded by a boot hum.
- Must match assets: `guashu-identity-board.png`.
- Drift bans: no limbs, no standing up, no monitor-shaped design, no illegible seed mass, no rotten or shrivelled cut face.

### The Grapes — console buttons (collective character)
- Identity one-liner: a bunch of grapes set into the console; each grape is a button; the labels fell off long ago so even they do not know which does what.
- Body / shape: full bunch of grapes embedded in the console panel, each showing its upper hemisphere.
- Default design: deep purple skins with bloom frost and specular highlight; each grape has a pair of small eyes and a small mouth.
- Series marker: at the tail of the bunch hangs one **shrivelled black grape with no facial features**, beside a faded label reading 绝对不要按 (DO NOT PRESS). It never speaks.
- Expression system: collective chorus. The pressed grape screams alone; the rest immediately shift blame to each other.
- Voice / speech texture: crowd of thin child voices in unison; the pressed one bursts into a single high scream. **Every episode ends on a grape scream hard-cut to black — the show's audio signature.**
- Must match assets: `grape-console-identity-board.png`.
- Drift bans: no green or red grapes (deep purple with frost only), no facial features on the black grape, no grapes detaching from the console.

### Jiaojiao — banana boat
- Identity one-liner: a boat made of banana peel that is afraid of water.
- Body / shape: one banana half-peeled; flesh side up as the hull, peeled-back skin as sail and gunwale.
- Default design: yellow with brown speckles in a fixed pattern (the speckle layout is an identity anchor); eyes and mouth at the bow.
- Series marker: fixed brown speckle pattern; deepens when afraid.
- Expression system: whole-body trembling when near water; brightens after being sung to.
- Signature mechanic: only enters the water after being sung to, which forces a delay beat at the top of act two.
- Voice / speech texture: warm simple teenage boy voice, slow; trembling when afraid; brighter tone once coaxed.
- Must match assets: `jiaojiao-identity-board.png`.
- Drift bans: no fully unpeeled banana, no shifting speckle layout, no willingly cheerful launch without the song.

### Mount Pineapple — volcano (scene / quasi-character)
- Identity one-liner: a mountain-sized pineapple that is the island's jam factory, heating plant and power station. **No language.**
- Body / shape: giant pineapple with a regular hexagonal scale grid and deep seams between scales; a huge green crown of leaves forms the summit vegetation; the crater sits at the centre of the crown.
- Default design: pineapple gold scales, deep seams, amber-gold jam. Before a burp the whole mass swells, the scale grid stretches apart, and internal gold light shows through the seams.
- Series marker: the hexagonal scale grid must stay regular — it is a pineapple, not a generic mountain.
- Expression system: no face, no mouth. All emotion via silhouette deformation, swelling, seam glow and sound.
- **Lighting lock: the volcano is the only warm light source on the island.** All night work is lit by the orange sweep of a burp crossing the island.
- Voice / speech texture: low rumble, burps, gurgles, sighs, held pressure. Good mood gives a full round burp; bad mood gives short broken burps; holding it in is a sustained low pressure tone.
- Must match assets: `volcano-identity-board.png`.
- Drift bans: no face or mouth, never speaks, no irregular scale grid, and **never red lava — ejecta is always amber-gold jam**.

### Laoha — cantaloupe bus
- Identity one-liner: the island's only bus line; permanently overloaded; blames the control centre for everything.
- Body / shape: long boxy bus body; cantaloupe netting reads as the aged bus panelling.
- Default design: pale green base with white netted ridges that fall exactly on the window and grille divisions; roof permanently loaded with strapped luggage; passengers squeezing out of window gaps.
- Series marker: the strapped roof luggage pile — silhouette may vary per episode but it must always be there.
- Expression system: irritation-first; leans on the horn.
- Signature mechanic: the horn is the dull thud of a ripe melon being slapped.
- Voice / speech texture: coarse hoarse middle-aged male voice; medium pace; every word barbed.
- Must match assets: `laoha-identity-board.png`.
- Drift bans: no smooth melon surface, no missing roof luggage, no netting that ignores the window divisions.

### Mangmang — mango sports car
- Identity one-liner: the island's only street racer; always speeding; natural enemy of the traffic light brothers.
- Body / shape: low streamlined mango body, tapered at the rear.
- Default design: orange-to-red gradient skin with the strongest specular reflection of any character in the show; narrow upswept eyes.
- Series marker: highest gloss in the cast.
- Expression system: cocky, loose, always amused.
- Signature mechanic: **mangoes are soft** — he dents on impact and leaks juice, then shakes once, reabsorbs the juice and is fine. Accidents cost him nothing, which is the physical reason he has no risk awareness.
- Voice / speech texture: flippant teenage male voice; fastest delivery in the cast; short clipped laughs.
- Must match assets: `mangmang-identity-board.png`.
- Drift bans: no hard undentable body, no permanent damage, no matte finish.

### The Three Brothers — fruit traffic light (collective character)
- Identity one-liner: three berries stacked on a sugarcane pole; all three claim to be the eldest and argue endlessly over whose turn it is to light.
- Body / shape: dark brown sugarcane pole with three fruits stacked top to bottom.
- Default design: **Red eldest is a strawberry, Yellow second is a kumquat, Green third is a green grape.** Each has a pair of small eyes and a mouth. Flesh backlights from inside when lit.
- Series marker: the fixed species trio — never substituted.
- Expression system: the three never share an expression; they are permanently glaring at each other.
- Signature mechanic: when the argument escalates all three light at once and the ring road instantly gridlocks. Natural disaster amplifier — no extra accident writing needed.
- Voice / speech texture: three distinct pitches. Red is the coarsest and most aggressive; Yellow is the shrillest and most impatient; Green is the slowest and most infuriating.
- Must match assets: `traffic-brothers-identity-board.png`.
- Drift bans: no species substitution, never detached from the pole, never a unified expression.

**Collective-character separation lock:** The Grapes are funny because they do not know their own function. The Three Brothers are funny because all three want to be in charge. Never blur these two comedy mechanics.

## Scenes

### Global
- Aspect: 16:9 landscape.
- Style source: Pixar-grade candy-colour 3D animation with physical fruit materiality; warm, bright, high saturation but soft.
- Texture signature (mandatory in every prompt): fruit wax highlights, water beads, translucent flesh, syrup stringing.
- Color card asset: `jam-island-color-card.png` — generate before writing 3+ video prompts.
- Practical-light rule: only scene-embedded sources — sky, volcano seam glow, jam glow in pipes, console screen glow, traffic light glow. No film softboxes or abstract studio lighting inside video prompts.
- **Master lighting lock:** the volcano is the only warm light source on the island. Night scenes are lit by the orange sweep of a burp.
- Scale rule: no humans anywhere. Fruit is the normal scale of this world; no size reference needed.
- Ejecta rule: all eruption material is amber-gold jam. Never red lava.
- Residents rule: Laoha, Mangmang and the Three Brothers appear **only** in the ring road junction scene. They never enter the control room — this keeps the main set from colour overload.

### Control Room Interior
- Time of day / weather: interior, lit by console glow plus volcano light through the viewing window.
- Key practical lights: Guashu's red cut-face screen, grape console glow, amber volcano light through the window.
- Spatial anchors: grape console downstage centre; Guashu embedded in the console behind it; the viewing window upstage with the volcano visible through it; Ningqi's post stage left, Bobo drifts stage right toward the console.
- Palette notes: grape purple console + watermelon green and red + amber window light. Bobo red and Ningqi yellow read as the two moving colour accents.
- Matching assets: `control-room-scene.png`.

### Volcano Crater
- Time of day / weather: exposed summit, heat haze.
- Key practical lights: gold light from inside the seams; the crater mouth itself.
- Spatial anchors: crater centre frame within the green crown; scale grid receding down the slope; pipe intakes at the lower rim.
- Palette notes: pineapple gold, deep seam shadow, amber jam, crown green.
- Matching assets: `volcano-crater-scene.png`.

### Island Panorama
- Time of day / weather: two locked variants — clear morning, and eruption state.
- Key practical lights: sky in morning state; volcano glow and glowing pipe network in eruption state.
- Spatial anchors: volcano dead centre; ring road circling the island; dock on the west, traffic junction on the east.
- Palette notes: morning is cool sky against warm island; eruption state pushes everything to amber.
- Matching assets: `island-panorama-morning-scene.png`, `island-panorama-eruption-scene.png`.

### Jam Dock
- Time of day / weather: morning, calm.
- Key practical lights: sky, amber shimmer off the jam sea surface.
- Spatial anchors: Jiaojiao moored parallel to the pier; pier leads back toward the volcano in depth.
- Palette notes: banana yellow against amber jam sea and pale dock stone.
- Matching assets: `jam-dock-scene.png`.

### Ring Road Junction
- Time of day / weather: daylight; the residents' standing stage.
- Key practical lights: sky plus the three-brother light glow.
- Spatial anchors: sugarcane light pole at frame right; Laoha's bus queued in the near lane; Mangmang cutting across the far lane; volcano visible on the horizon behind.
- Palette notes: cantaloupe pale-green and mango orange against grey road; red/yellow/green light accents.
- Matching assets: `ring-road-junction-scene.png`.

### Fireworks Night Sky (Ep 10 only)
- Time of day / weather: night, clear and cold at altitude.
- Key practical lights: the sugar blooms themselves plus volcano glow from below.
- Spatial anchors: residents parked along the ring road looking up; blooms fill the upper two thirds of frame.
- Palette notes: amber and gold blooms against deep blue night — the only cool-dominant frame in the series.
- Matching assets: `fireworks-night-sky-scene.png`.
- Note: single-use special asset, but it is the cover shot for all season marketing.

## Pacing

- Form: landscape slapstick adventure comedy, self-contained episodes.
- Unit length: about 4 minutes; hard ceiling 4 minutes 30 seconds.
- Prompt segment target: 10-15 seconds per generated clip, then edit together (about 18-24 clips per episode).
- Fixed skeleton: seven beats — burp open / assignment / button press / rescue farce / bargain low point / absurd solution / callback close. See `series-bible.md` §3.2.
- One-variable rule: the only thing that changes between episodes is which grape gets pressed. Characters, scenes, runners and structure stay fixed.
- Hook window: the 20-second burp open is fixed and reused; the episode's actual problem must land by 1:10.
- Mid beat cadence: a new visual beat every 5-8 seconds; no extended static dialogue.
- Cost-of-damage rule: act two must cut to the ring road junction and show the disaster landing on Laoha and Mangmang.
- Climax rule: the solution in beat six must come from an existing character's flaw becoming an advantage. **Never invent a new prop to solve the problem.**
- Cliffhanger: not a plot cliffhanger — every episode closes on Bobo eyeing the next grape and that grape screaming as the picture cuts to black.
- Genre lane: slapstick physical comedy. Not educational, not science explainer, not drama.
- Emotion arc: routine → assignment → transgression → escalation → collapse → absurd rescue → unchanged appetite.
- Composition lock: 16:9 landscape; two-lead dialogue uses shot-reverse-shot off the console; keep the volcano visible in a window or on the horizon whenever plausible so the threat is never off-screen.
- Dialogue budget: short lines. Bobo and Mangmang fastest, Ningqi tightest, Guashu slowest. The volcano has no dialogue at all.
- Externalisation rule: no interior emotion on screen. Every feeling must be rendered as a physical, filmable consequence.
- BGM rule: video-generation prompts must suppress BGM; music is designed in post.
